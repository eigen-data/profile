01 — Consulting

Data, Statistics, Research & Analytics

We help you clean, analyze, and interpret data so you can make better decisions — whether for academic research, business insight, or organizational reporting.

What we offer

  • Data cleaning & preparation (Excel, CSV, SPSS, etc.)
  • Descriptive & exploratory analysis
  • Statistical analysis (regression, ANOVA, non-parametric, etc.)
  • Multivariate analysis & SEM (SmartPLS, AMOS, R)
  • Spatial analysis (QGIS, ArcGIS, R Spatial)
  • Machine learning models (Python)
  • Research consultation & method selection
  • Data visualization & reporting
